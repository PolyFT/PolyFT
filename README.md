## About

I study how polymer structure and composition govern degradation, combustion, safety, and sustainability. The research integrates polymer chemistry, real-time combustion analysis, scientific instrumentation, and artificial intelligence, with the aim of moving materials research from empirical formulation toward mechanism-informed design.

Current work focuses on composition–mechanism–performance modeling across thermoplastics, cross-linked polymers, and polymer composites. Multimodal combustion data are organized around four mechanistic stages: pyrolysis, volatile evolution, gas-phase oxidation, and char formation.

## Research

- **Polymer intelligence** — Unified polymer representations, evidence-linked literature data, interpretable structure–mechanism–property models, and AI-assisted materials design.
- **Fire-safe polymeric materials** — Molecular and formulation design of flame-retardant, intrinsically safe, thermal-protection, and high-performance polymer systems.
- **Combustion science and instrumentation** — Real-time, in-situ analysis of polymer combustion, coupled optical–mass-spectrometric measurements, and mechanism-resolved datasets.
- **Sensing and sustainable polymers** — Early-fire perception systems and recyclable or biomass-derived polymers designed for coordinated safety, performance, and circularity.
