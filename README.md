I study how polymer structure, composition, degradation, and combustion interact across multiple scales. My group combines **polymer chemistry**, **fire science**, **in-situ combustion analysis**, and **artificial intelligence** to develop safer and more sustainable polymeric materials.

Our work connects scientific understanding with practical systems—from molecular and formulation design to combustion instrumentation and early-fire warning devices.

## Research

- **AI for polymer materials** — polymer representation, literature data extraction, structure–mechanism–property modeling, and data-driven materials design
- **Fire-safe polymeric materials** — molecular and formulation design of flame-retardant, intrinsically safe, and high-performance polymers
- **Polymer combustion science** — real-time analysis of pyrolysis, volatile evolution, gas-phase oxidation, and char formation
- **Early-fire detection** — responsive materials, sensing devices, and intelligent warning systems for latent and incipient fires
- **Sustainable polymers** — recyclable and biomass-derived polymers with integrated safety and performance
